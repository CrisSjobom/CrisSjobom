### Hello, I'm Cris.

I build at the intersection of **technical product leadership, software engineering, and operational complexity**.

Over more than two decades in technology — including more than eight years in product management — I've learned that the most valuable solutions come from understanding the problem deeply, working closely with engineering, and staying accountable for what happens in the real world.

Today, I'm a **Technical Product Manager at Mottu**, working on systems that support field services and motorcycle logistics. My background also includes product work in financial technology, across **XP, Rico, and Clear**.

I haven't stopped being curious about how software is built. In fact, I'm spending more time hands-on again: writing code, experimenting with AI-assisted development, and turning ideas into working tools.

> **My north star:** Understand the operation. Design the right system. Build with purpose. Measure what changes.

### What I'm exploring

- **AI-powered workflows:** where agents and automation can reduce repetitive work and strengthen decision-making.
- **Optimization problems:** how routing, constraints, and resource allocation can be modeled in software.
- **Context graphs:** how relationships among entities, events, and decisions can improve system intelligence.
- **Building with AI:** using modern development tools to move from an idea to a useful, maintainable solution.

### Principles I build by

**Start with the real problem.** Elegant architecture only matters if it solves something meaningful.

**Stay technically curious.** Product leadership and hands-on engineering make each other stronger.

**Design for the people operating the system.** The best solutions respect the reality of day-to-day work.

**Prefer outcomes over theater.** Shipping is important; understanding impact is essential.
